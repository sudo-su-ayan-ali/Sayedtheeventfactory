SECTION 3: VISUAL DIRECTION & AESTHETIC
-----------------------------------------------------------------

THEME: "Dark Luxury 3D Spectacular"

PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE:
- Deep Black / Charcoal: #0A0A0A / #111111 (main background)
- Rich Gold: #C9A84C / #E2B96F (primary accent, headings)
- Warm Ivory/Cream: #F5ECD7 (body text, softer contrast)
- Deep Crimson/Maroon: #8B1A1A (secondary accent, Rajasthani touch)
- Electric Champagne: #FFE4A0 (highlight glow effects)

WHY THIS PALETTE?
- Black + Gold = luxury, premium, event industry standard
- Crimson = Rajasthani cultural identity, celebration, vibrancy
- Ivory = softness, elegance, human warmth
- Together they feel: royal, Indian, premium, bold

TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION:
- Display / Hero Font: Something dramatic and editorial.
  Suggest using "Playfair Display" or "Cormorant Garamond"
  for large headings — these feel theatrical and ceremonial.
- Subheading Font: "DM Serif Display" or "Libre Baskerville"
  for mid-level titles.
- Body Font: "Lato" or "Nunito" — clean, readable, warm.
- Accent / Label Font: A thin geometric sans-serif for
  labels, tags, and UI elements.

OVERALL AESTHETIC KEYWORDS:
Grand | Theatrical | Royal | Immersive | 3D | Indian Luxury
Celebratory | Cinematic | Dark Background | Gold Glow

SECTION 4: 3D DESIGN ELEMENTS TO INCLUDE
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The "3D look" of this website will be achieved through
the following techniques (pure CSS/JS — no heavy 3D engines
needed unless specifically requested):

4.1 HERO SECTION — 3D PARALLAX
- A multi-layered hero background that moves as you scroll
  or move your mouse. Different elements (confetti, lights,
  venue silhouettes) float at different depths.
- The business name "SAYED" appears in large 3D-extruded
  gold text with a soft drop shadow and light reflection.
- "THE EVENT FACTORY" appears below in a slightly smaller,
  lighter weight with a glowing underline.

4.2 FLOATING CARDS — SERVICE SECTIONS
- Each service (Wedding, Corporate, Birthday, etc.) is shown
  on a card that has a 3D tilt effect on hover.
- When you hover over a card, it lifts towards you, casts
  a deeper shadow, and reveals a glowing gold border.
- Cards have subtle depth layers — foreground icon,
  mid-ground title, background gradient.

4.3 STATS / NUMBERS SECTION
- Animated count-up numbers (e.g., 500+ Events, 8 Years, etc.)
- Each number sits inside a 3D-effect badge/emblem
- Soft particle/sparkle animations float around them

4.4 GALLERY / PORTFOLIO
- A 3D carousel or stacked card layout showing past events
- Photos appear to be layered, with depth and shadow
- On hover, the selected image "pops out" towards the viewer

4.5 TESTIMONIALS
- Floating quote bubbles with soft 3D shadow
- Client name and event type shown below
- Subtle background rotation animation

4.6 CONTACT / CTA SECTION
- A dark, atmospheric section with glowing gold CTA button
- The button has a 3D press effect when clicked
- Background has soft bokeh lights / particle animation

SECTION 6: ANIMATIONS & INTERACTIONS PLAN
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The following animations will make the site feel alive and 3D:

ON PAGE LOAD:
- The hero headline letters animate in one by one (stagger)
- Background particles slowly float upward
- Logo fades in with a gold glow pulse

ON SCROLL:
- Each section fades + slides up as it enters the viewport
- The stats section triggers count-up animation when visible
- Parallax layers in hero move at different scroll speeds

ON HOVER:
- Service cards tilt in 3D (CSS perspective transform)
- Portfolio images scale up and cast deeper shadows
- Navigation links get gold underline that sweeps in
- All buttons glow softly and "press" on click

CURSOR EFFECT (OPTIONAL):
- A custom gold dot cursor that follows the mouse
- Leaves a short trailing glow as it moves
- Expands when hovering over interactive elements

BACKGROUND EFFECTS:
- Floating bokeh light circles (slow CSS animation)
- Subtle noise grain texture overlay (adds film feel)
- Occasional sparkle/confetti particle burst in hero

SECTION 8: TECHNICAL STACK RECOMMENDATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------

For a 3D, animated, production-ready website:

OPTION A (Recommended — Pure HTML/CSS/JS):
- HTML5 + CSS3 (CSS variables, transforms, animations)
- Vanilla JavaScript for scroll animations, parallax,
  cursor effects, and count-up numbers
- No heavy dependencies — fast loading, SEO friendly
- Can be hosted on any basic hosting (Hostinger, GoDaddy, etc.)

OPTION B (React-based — More Dynamic):
- React.js with Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion for animations
- Three.js (optional) for true 3D elements
- Better for future scalability (adding a booking form, etc.)

FOR THIS PROJECT: We will build Option A first — it gives the
richest visual result without requiring complex infrastructure.
