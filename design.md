-----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Sayed The Event Factory is an event planning and management business.
The brand should feel:
- Premium, sleek, and highly professional
- Minimalist and modern

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: THE CORE CONCEPT / BIG IDEA
-----------------------------------------------------------------

CONCEPT TITLE: "Cinematic Minimalist (Apple Pro Style)"

The experience should feel like an Apple product page:
- Clean, content-first layout.
- High-contrast typography.
- Smooth, subtle scroll animations (fades and scales).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: VISUAL DIRECTION & AESTHETIC
-----------------------------------------------------------------

THEME: "Apple Pro Cinematic"

PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE:
- Background: Pure Black (#000000) and Deep Gray (#111111)
- Text: Off-white (#f5f5f7) and Silver/Gray (#86868b)
- Accents: Subtle Gold (#C9A84C) and Crimson (#8B1A1A) for highlights.

TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION:
- Font: Inter (or San Francisco on Apple devices).
- Headings: Large, bold, tight letter-spacing.
- Body: Clean, high legibility, smaller font sizes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: 3D / ANIMATION ELEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------

- Replaced heavy 3D elements with subtle intersection observer reveals (fading up and scaling slightly).
- Used `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` for the frosted glass navbar effect.
- Removed particles and custom cursors to reduce noise and enhance the premium, minimalist feel.
